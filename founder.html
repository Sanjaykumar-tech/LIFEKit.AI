Why I built this

In 2024, I watched my cousin spend two full weekends making her marriage biodata in MS Word. Formatting. Re-formatting. Sending to her mom for approval. Getting it back. Formatting again.

Then I watched her friend do the exact same thing a month later.

Same problem. Same time wasted. Same bad output.

I thought: why is this still hard in 2025? You fill a form, you get a beautiful PDF. That's it. It shouldn't take 4 hours.

Most people don't need better software. They need software that solves one small thing, really well.

The problem with existing tools

Most "biodata makers" online are:

  • Ugly — look like they were designed in 2008
  • Expensive — ₹500+/month subscription for one PDF
  • Confusing — sign up, verify email, choose plan, blah blah
  • Full of ads — or force you to download random software

So I built the opposite: clean, fast, one-time payment, no signup.

How LIFEKit AI works

Three simple tools:

  • 💍 Biodata Maker — marriage biodata with 3 templates (₹99)
  • 💼 Resume Maker — ATS-friendly resume (₹49)
  • ❤️ Wishes Maker — birthday / love / anniversary cards (₹19)

Each does one thing well. Fill a form → preview live → download a PDF or image.

No signup. No monthly fee. No tracking beyond basic analytics. Just fill, preview, download.
