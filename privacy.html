Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — plain English, no lawyer talk.

1. What we collect

We collect almost nothing. LIFEKit AI runs entirely in your browser. When you fill a form, the data stays on your device — it's never sent to our servers.

The only data we collect is:

  • Payment info (name, email) — handled securely by Razorpay
  • Anonymous usage stats — via Google Analytics (page views only)
  • Contact form submissions — if you choose to email us

2. What we don't collect

  • Your biodata content — stays in your browser
  • Your resume content — stays in your browser
  • Your wish card messages — stays in your browser
  • Uploaded photos — processed locally, never uploaded

3. Payments

All payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card details. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to payment data.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for analytics (Google Analytics). We also use browser localStorage to remember if you've already paid for a tool. You can clear this anytime from your browser settings.

5. Third parties

  • Razorpay — payments
  • Google Analytics — anonymous stats
  • Netlify — website hosting
  • Google Fonts — fonts

6. Your rights

You can request deletion of any data we hold by emailing sanjaykumarnov12@gmail.com. Since we hold very little data, this is usually instant.

7. Children

LIFEKit AI is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions? Email sanjaykumarnov12@gmail.com.
